Paramount CBS Studio 2025

LED volumne to Extended Reality is one of the most fast growing tech sectors in the Media entertainment business. Paramount CBS Studio produced a series of graphics from LED wall to Augmented Reality and Mixed Reality.

CBSNEWS / PARAMOUNT

My work focused on designing spatially coherent, data driven 3D spaces for live broadcast and streaming media. I developed reusable 2D/3D graphic systems, interactive studio elements, and real-time workflows that allowed shows to scale across networks and regions while maintaining visual and system consistency. From CBS Evening News and CBS Mornings LED volume Studios to CBS Virtual Sports Studios, Paramount Brazil Sports Studio, CBS News and Stations, Weather Sphere Studio, Inside Edition, and CBS News Stream Virtual Studios, I led both the creative design and technical integration of real-time virtual studio environments. On the technical side, I built and integrated real-time data pipelines and GPU based rendering architectures, enabling live data to drive visuals reliably and efficiently. Working closely with editorial, production, and engineering teams, I helped translate creative ideas into stable, high performance systems for live television.
